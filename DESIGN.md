## Process

How to decide.

We have levels, and two modifiers:

*climax* - 1 page : intros, centerfold, and finales
*epic*   - 1/2 of page, 1/3 or 1/4 of page
*normal* - 1 fill cell 1/6, can expand to 2 (1/3) as needed to fill gaps

Modifiers:

*vista*  - make wider than high
*action* - turn 1 image into 3 panes for "motion" effect

For comic books must flow properly and fill the page based on a *stream* of the above *tags*.

For regular picture books, left-to-right and down narrative flow does not apply.
